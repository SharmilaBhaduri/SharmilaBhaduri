### 🌸 Hi, I'm Sharmila Bhaduri!

I am a Computer Applications graduate with an analytical foundation, a creative mindset, and a strong interest in exploring the intersection of **Technology, Communication, Data, and Storytelling.**

I believe that technology is not just about writing code. It is also about understanding problems, organizing information, communicating ideas, and creating solutions that people can actually use.

My journey combines two worlds that might seem different at first:

- 💻 **The Logical World:** Programming, computer applications, AI concepts, image processing, databases, and digital technology.
- ✍️ **The Creative World:** Fiction writing, screenplay development, storytelling, dialogue design, presentation creation, and visual communication.

I enjoy transforming complicated ideas into understandable information, raw data into meaningful presentations, and imaginative concepts into structured stories.
